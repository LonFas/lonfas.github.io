## Secure Communication

Sometimes I use **Tox** too - it is a decentralized, encrypted instant messaging protocol designed to keep your data private. While not widely popular, it has been working for over 10 years.

### Why Tox?

* **Absolute Privacy**: No central servers that can be blocked, raided, or compromised.
* **True Security**: All traffic is fully end-to-end encrypted using reliable cryptographic libraries.
* **Freedom**: It is completely free, open-source, and contains no corporate tracking or ads.
